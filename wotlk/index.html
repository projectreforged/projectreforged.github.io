What is Project Reforged
The Project

High Definition.
Faithful to the Original.

Project Reforged is a long-term technical initiative dedicated to raising the visual fidelity of the World of Warcraft® 3.3.5 client — without sacrificing its atmosphere.

It involves restructuring, converting, and integrating high-definition visual elements so they behave naturally inside the original engine, prioritizing stability, immersion, and a coherent, faithful look.
